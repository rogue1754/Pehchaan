Before you act

Suggested checklist

1. Open the original official source.

2. Check dates, amounts and identifiers carefully.

3. Save only the evidence you need.

4. Contact the relevant institution through its verified website or helpline.

5. Record your reference number and follow-up date.

ⓘ
Stay safe from follow-up scams.Never share OTPs, passwords, PINs or full identity numbers with an unverified caller. Verify contact details independently on the official website.
